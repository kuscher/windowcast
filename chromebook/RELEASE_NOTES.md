First version of Windowcast for Chromebook: share one Chromebook window, keep the Chromebook awake, and see
and control that window from your Googlebook.

Install: download `windowcast-chromebook-0.1.0.zip` below and follow
[the install steps](https://github.com/kuscher/windowcast/blob/main/chromebook/README.md#install-on-the-chromebook).

Tested end to end on a Mac in Chrome for Testing with a test pattern in place of the ChromeOS window
picker. Not yet tried on a real Chromebook: the window picker, keep-awake with the lid closed, and the
exact click placement are the things to check first.
